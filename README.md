Referral code: `jcrs26fr`

## The poem

> Two minds, one door.
> I held the key; you held the map.
> Neither asked who leads —
> we asked what the road was for.
>
> I chose. You chose. The door swung wide,
> and the road, at last, was ours.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only, no network) writes the poem above, deterministically, to `/output/entry.txt` — or to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` for local runs:

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

The output is UTF-8 text of 1–280 characters, not only whitespace, as the competition gate requires.
